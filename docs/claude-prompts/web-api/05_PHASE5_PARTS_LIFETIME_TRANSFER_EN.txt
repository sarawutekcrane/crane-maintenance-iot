IMPORTANT:

1. The browser must never read/write Google Sheets directly.
2. The Web must call the Backend API.
3. ESP32 devices must call the Backend/Device API, not Google Sheets.
4. Business logic belongs in backend/domain services, not spreadsheet formulas.
5. The repository layer isolates Google Sheets from business logic.
6. Replacing Google Sheets with PostgreSQL later must not require rewriting Web workflows.
7. API contracts must be versioned and stable.
8. Historical records must not be overwritten.
9. Revision-controlled masters must preserve old historical meaning.
10. Do not fabricate missing real-world data.

QR RULE
======================================================================

One crane must have ONE permanent QR code.

QR target:

/vehicle/{vehicle_id}

The QR opens Vehicle Detail, not a dedicated inspection-only page.

Workshop equipment may use:

/equipment/{equipment_id}

and one permanent QR per machine.

ACCEPTANCE TESTS

1. Consumable works without part_instance.
2. Position lifetime works without serializing every unit.
3. Instance tracking can start on demand.
4. Known prior usage can be entered.
5. Unknown history stays UNKNOWN.
6. Transfer does not reset accumulated usage.
7. IN_REPAIR does not accumulate vehicle operating hours.
8. normal repair does not reset lifetime.
9. approved overhaul can create new tracking cycle.
10. old cycles remain.
11. same name/different specification can be separate part IDs.
12. no requirement exists to preload every physical part.
